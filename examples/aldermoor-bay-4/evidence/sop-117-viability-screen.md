# SOP-117 Rev 6: Cell viability screen (384-well)

Owner: Screening Operations. Effective 2025-02-03.

## Purpose
Dose-response viability screen. One plate runs the full sequence below without operator contact.

## Sequence
| # | Step | Log name | Instrument class | Nominal time |
| - | ---- | -------- | ---------------- | ------------ |
| 1 | Plate out | plate-out | Plate hotel | 30 s |
| 2 | Compound transfer | compound-transfer | Liquid handler | 240 s |
| 3 | Seal | seal | Sealer | 18 s |
| 4 | Incubate | incubate | Incubator | 30 min |
| 5 | Spin | spin | Centrifuge | 120 s |
| 6 | Peel | peel | Peeler | 22 s |
| 7 | Reagent add | reagent-add | Dispenser | 75 s |
| 8 | Read | read | Plate reader | 8 min |
| 9 | Image | image | High-content imager | 7 min |
| 10 | Archive | archive | -20C store | 30 s |

## Transfers
The arm carries the plate between all stations, including onto the imager stage.

## Notes
Plates must not wait more than 45 minutes unsealed after step 6.
